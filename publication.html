Publish Your Research with APJMBB

The MTERMS 2026 Organizing Committee is pleased to invite authors to submit full manuscripts for publication consideration in the MTERMS 2026 Special Issue of APJMBB. This is an excellent opportunity to elevate your research and share your findings with a broader scientific community.

Submission Deadline 31 August 2026
Processing Fee RM500
Only upon acceptance
Expected Publication March 2027

Submission and Author Guidelines

To support a smooth review process, all manuscripts must be prepared strictly in accordance with the journal's formatting and author requirements.

  • Prepare the full manuscript according to the APJMBB formatting standards.
  • Submit the manuscript through the official APJMBB online submission portal.
  • Select “MTERM 2026 Special Issue” as the manuscript type.
  • Ensure that all author details, declarations, figures and references are complete.

Publication Process

All submissions will undergo a rigorous peer-review process coordinated by the MTERMS Editorial Team and appointed expert reviewers. Publication is subject to successful peer review and final editorial acceptance.

STEP 2Editorial Screening

Publication Processing Fee

A publication processing fee of RM500 applies only after the manuscript has been accepted. Authors will not be invoiced at the initial submission or peer-review stage.

Visit the APJMBB submission portal and select “MTERM 2026 Special Issue” under Manuscript Type.
