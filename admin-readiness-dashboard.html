Presentation Readiness Definition

Oral — Ready: The participant is confirmed as an Oral presenter and has uploaded at least one slides file. The MTERMS upload system accepts Oral slides in PDF format.
Oral — Pending: The participant is confirmed as an Oral presenter but no slides file has been uploaded.

Poster — Ready: The participant is confirmed as a Poster presenter and has uploaded at least one poster file.
Poster — Pending: The participant is confirmed as a Poster presenter but no poster file has been uploaded.

Participants who are not currently assigned as Oral or Poster are not included in Presentation Readiness.
