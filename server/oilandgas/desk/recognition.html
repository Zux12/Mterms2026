Evidence-aware AI paper review

Check one published paper against Crossref (where a DOI exists), then request a bounded AI assessment using only verified bibliographic data and the stored paper summary. The result is advisory only; it cannot activate recognition, issue a certificate or send an email. AI configuration is required on the backend.



Documented integrity approval (testing)


Only an already reviewed publication with matching Crossref metadata can be approved for the future automatic shortlist. Check the original publisher's status and reliable correction/retraction notices first; record the public evidence and editorial rationale. This does not feature the paper, issue a certificate, or send an email.











Administrator override
Stage a replacement paper for one recognition category. Changes are audited and applied at activation; staging itself does not change the website.










Controlled activation
Only activate a weekly selection after checking the candidate publications and their authors. Activation publishes recognition data to its own public endpoint and archive, but does not send emails.




Withdraw invalid recognition
A withdrawn selection is removed from active and archive feeds. Previously issued certificates are invalidated, but verification remains available and shows the withdrawn status. No emails are sent.


Co-author recognition letters


Issue a formal A4 Letter of Recognition only after confirming a co-author's exact published byline name. Letters are verifiable and can be printed or saved as PDF. No email will be sent.
