Source and research-integrity clearance

Independent of the keyword score, an editor can provide a source-integrity verification URL and documented rationale. Full abstracts are not required. Scoring never establishes scientific integrity by itself.

Related research screening — editorial caution

Find high-scoring papers with highly similar titles and author lists across different publication sources. Such similarities require an editor to compare the actual publications; they are not evidence of misconduct and do not automatically disqualify either paper. No recognition or email is triggered here.

Optional full-paper research evidence (exception review)

When abstract evidence is insufficient, an editor may optionally review the original paper and supply grounded evidence of its methodology, results, limitations and industry implications. A title or abstract alone is insufficient. The saved evidence is never made public by this form.

The result is advisory only; it cannot activate recognition, issue a certificate or send an email. AI configuration is required on the backend.

Controlled activation
Only activate a weekly selection after checking the candidate publications and their authors. Activation publishes recognition data to its own public endpoint and archive, but does not send emails.




Withdraw invalid recognition
A withdrawn selection is removed from active and archive feeds. Previously issued certificates are invalidated, but verification remains available and shows the withdrawn status. No emails are sent.
